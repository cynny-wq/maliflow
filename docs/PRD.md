# MaliFlow Product Requirements

## Problem

SME owners struggle to know:

- Who owes them money
- What they owe suppliers
- Daily cash position
- Future cash flow

## Goal

Build the simplest cash flow platform for SMEs.

## Version 1 Features

- Dashboard
- Cash In
- Cash Out
- Customer Debts
- Reports

## Future Features

- Forecasting
- Notifications
- Multi-user
- Analytics
